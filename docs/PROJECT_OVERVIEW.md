# CarbonRoot Project Overview

## What CarbonRoot Is
CarbonRoot is a carbon credit buying and retirement platform. It helps buyers source verified credits from listed projects, retire them on-chain, and receive audit-ready proof.

The product is trust-first, not crypto-first. The key value is:

`verified supply -> simple purchase -> permanent retirement -> clear proof`

## Primary Users
- Enterprise sustainability, ESG, and compliance teams
- Smaller businesses that need a simpler offset purchase flow

## Core Problems We Solve
- Carbon credits are often hard to trust
- Buying through brokers is slow and manual
- Buyers need proof they can show in reporting and audits

## Product Positioning
- CarbonRoot should be presented as a verified procurement and retirement platform
- Credits are treated as a delivery instrument for real-world carbon projects
- The product should not be framed as speculative, DeFi-first, or investment-led

## Product Modes

### Compliance Mode
Built for enterprise buyers who need:
- clearer records
- bulk purchasing
- a more structured retirement workflow
- audit-ready proof

### Simple Mode
Built for smaller buyers who need:
- project discovery
- quick purchase
- quick retirement
- a clean certificate outcome

## Current Demo Scope
The current live demo goal is one complete public testnet flow on Base Sepolia:

1. Connect wallet
2. Approve MockUSDT
3. Buy CRT
4. Retire CRT
5. View the certificate page with real transaction hashes

## What Matters Most In The Demo
- The listed project feels credible
- The purchase flow feels simple
- The retirement is permanent
- The certificate is the proof moment

## What We Deliberately Avoid
- speculative token messaging
- governance token language
- yield-first positioning
- heavy Web3 jargon in user-facing copy
- unnecessary feature sprawl in the demo

## Product Voice
- precise
- credible
- quietly ambitious

Avoid words like:
- revolutionary
- guaranteed
- investment
